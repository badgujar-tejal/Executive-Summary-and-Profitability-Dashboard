# Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard (Excel)

A dynamic and interactive **Excel Dashboard** built to transform raw global sales data into clean, actionable business insights. This repository contains the analytics assets tracking global metrics, regional performances, and core business KPIs.


## 📊 Business Performance Summary
Based on the analyzed dataset, the dashboard highlights key metrics regarding the company's financial health:
* **High-Value Revenue:** Generated **$645,223 in Total Revenue** across all regions.
* **Strong Profitability:** Maintained an impressive **45.94% Gross Profit Margin**, bringing in **$296,384 in Total Gross Profit**.
* **Order & Volume Tracking:** Successfully processed **2,707 total orders** and moved **6,698 total units**.
* **Efficient Cost Structure:** **Total COGS** (Cost of Goods Sold) was managed at **$348,839**.

## 📈 Key Insights Captured
* **Sales Cycles:** Identifies clear performance peaks in **June ($70K)** and **November ($66K)**, alongside early-year slumps in February ($36K).
* **Top Geographic Markets:** Tracks leading European markets, with the **United Kingdom ($74K)** and **Germany ($70K)** driving the highest revenue.
* **Dominant Channels:** Highlights **Online Stores as the primary sales driver**, bringing in **40%** of overall revenue.

## 🛠️ Features & Tools Used
* **Data Modeling & Pivot Tables:** Synthesized complex sales records into clean, structured summaries.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Enabled instantaneous visual filtering by Product Category, Quarter, or Country.
* **Advanced Charting:** Utilized dual-line charts, ranked bar graphs, geo-maps, and doughnut charts for regional comparisons.
